Ingredients

  • 600g tofu
  • 100g pork or beef mince
  • 20g canola oil
  • 25g garlic
  • 20g ginger
  • 25g broad bean paste
  • 1tsp soy sauce
  • 2tsp chilli powder
  • 65ml water
  • 2tsp corchstarch

Steps

  1. Put oil in the pan and heat
  2. Once oil is heated, put mince in pan and cook until no longer red
  3. Put ground garlic, ginger, chilli powder and soy sauce in to pan and mix
  4. While cooking, chop your tofu up in to cubes and put them in the pan, mix well
  5. Add water and cornstarch in to the pan and mix until gluggy.
  6. Enjoy!
