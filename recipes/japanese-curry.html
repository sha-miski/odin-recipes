Ingredients

  • 400g onion - sliced
  • 250g potato - cut in to cubes
  • 100g carrot - sliced
  • 1tbsp canola oil
  • 3-5 cubes of Golden Curry
  • 4.5 cups of water
  • 4 cups of rice
  • 4 chicken cutlets

Steps

  1. Cook chicken cutlets separately until done.
  2. Add oil to a hot pot.
  3. Add onion and saute until browned.
  4. Add potatoes and carrots in to the pot and stir until slightly cooked.
  5. Add water and turn heat up, bring it to a boil, then reduce heat.
  6. Add cubes of Golden Curry one at a time, mix each one before adding the next.
  7. Reduce heat to low, place lid on and cook for 10-15 minutes or until curry is dissolved. Stir occasionally.
  8. Add water if curry is too thick.
  9. Enjoy!
