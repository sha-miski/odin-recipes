Banana Bread

Ingredients

  • 4-5 Large Bananas.
  • 120g Brown sugar.
  • 120g Butter (Melted).
  • 2-3 Large eggs.
  • 1tsp Vanilla exract.
  • 180g All-purpose flour.
  • 1tsp baking soda.
  • 1/2tsp salt.
  • 1/2tsp cinnamon.
  • Extras: 120g nut of choice, and or chocolate.

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180C.
  2. Wet ingredients: in a large bowl; mash the bananas then mix in eggs, sugar, melted butter and vanilla.
  3. Dry ingredients: in a medium bowl; whisk together flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon.
  4. Mix wet and dry ingredients together, fold in your extra of choice.
  5. Prepare the bread pan, pour in the batter and top with more extras.
  6. Bake for about 50 minutes or until ready. Generally I found this to be around 40-50 minutes, but it depends.
  7. Enjoy!
